# DoraHacks submission copy

## Vision
Make cross-chain settlement provable, programmable, and hard to corrupt.

## Project detail
ProofSettle turns verified blockchain events into real settlement decisions. A supplier completes a milestone on Sepolia; Attestcoin proves the event; a Creditcoin policy checks chain, emitter, event, order and amount; then settlement executes once. AI explains and recommends, but never becomes the trust boundary. Judges can inspect the evidence, policy gates, rejection path, and on-chain trail.
